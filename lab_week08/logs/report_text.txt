2. What pattern appears in the positional encoding heatmap? For pair index k, ωk = exp[−2k log(10000)/dmodel ], with
PE(p, 2k) = sin(pωk ) and PE(p, 2k + 1) = cos(pωk ). In the actual 100 × 128 heatmap below, low-index dimensions oscillate
rapidly as sequence position changes. Higher-index dimensions have lower frequencies and longer wavelengths, so their colors
vary slowly over the 100 positions. Adjacent dimensions form sine/cosine pairs with the same frequency and a phase offset. At
position zero they alternate between zero and one; far to the right, the sine channels stay near zero and the cosine channels near
one, producing nearly constant vertical stripes. This multiscale encoding is added to the input embeddings without changing
their shape. The separate attention heatmap shows row-wise probability mass; independent random Q and K do not imply a
diagonal pattern.
